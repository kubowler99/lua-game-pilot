# Prioritized Roadmap for 2D Mobile Game Development (Solar2D)

This roadmap prioritizes game concepts across **Low**, **Medium**, and **High Effort** tiers. The order is based on ease of entry for a solo developer, opportunities to learn core game development skills, and potential market appeal.

---

## Phase 1: Low Effort (1–2 Months)

### 1. **Puzzle Blocks** (Match-3 Puzzle)
- **Why First:** Well-documented mechanics, strong casual market appeal, minimal art requirements.
- **Learning Benefits:** Teaches grid management, cascading logic, and UI polish.
- **AI Leverage:** Generate puzzle layouts, art assets, and difficulty tuning.

### 2. **Pixel Runner** (Endless Runner)
- **Why Second:** Simple mechanics, quick to prototype, good for learning collision and scrolling.
- **Learning Benefits:** Introduces physics, collision detection, and procedural variation.
- **AI Leverage:** Background art, obstacle patterns.

### 3. **Tap Rhythm Hero** (Rhythm/Tap Game)
- **Why Third:** Slightly more complex due to audio sync, but builds timing and input precision skills.
- **Learning Benefits:** Audio integration, beat mapping, scoring systems.
- **AI Leverage:** Procedural beat maps, music composition.

---

## Phase 2: Medium Effort (3–6 Months)

### 1. **Tower Defense Chronicles**
- **Why First in Medium:** Popular genre, manageable scope, strong replayability.
- **Learning Benefits:** Pathfinding, wave management, balancing difficulty.
- **AI Leverage:** Procedural wave generation, balancing curves.

### 2. **Mystic Farm** (Casual Farming Simulator)
- **Why Second:** Expands into resource management and progression systems.
- **Learning Benefits:** Time-based events, economy balancing, inventory systems.
- **AI Leverage:** Seasonal events, crop art, quest text.

### 3. **Quest of the Tiny Kingdom** (Adventure/RPG Lite)
- **Why Third:** Larger scope with maps and combat, requires more content pipelines.
- **Learning Benefits:** Map navigation, combat mechanics, inventory systems.
- **AI Leverage:** Quest text, NPC dialogue, map layouts.

---

## Phase 3: High Effort (6+ Months)

### 1. **Chrono Puzzle Saga** (Narrative Puzzle Adventure)
- **Why First in High:** Narrative-driven puzzle games are niche but achievable with strong AI support.
- **Learning Benefits:** Story branching logic, puzzle variety, save/load states.
- **AI Leverage:** Narrative generation, puzzle design, art assets.

### 2. **Legends of the Lost Realm** (Action RPG)
- **Why Second:** Ambitious but rewarding; builds on prior RPG-lite experience.
- **Learning Benefits:** Procedural generation, combat systems, persistence.
- **AI Leverage:** Dungeon generation, quest writing, NPC dialogue.

### 3. **Galactic Traders Online** (Multiplayer Trading/Strategy)
- **Why Last:** Requires networking, multiplayer systems, and server-side logic.
- **Learning Benefits:** Multiplayer synchronization, dynamic economy, social features.
- **AI Leverage:** Trade events, economy balancing, NPC traders.

---

## Summary

- **Start Small:** Begin with **Puzzle Blocks** to learn core mechanics and ship a market-ready game quickly.
- **Build Complexity:** Progress to **Tower Defense Chronicles** for deeper systems and replayability.
- **Go Ambitious:** Tackle **Chrono Puzzle Saga** or **Legends of the Lost Realm** once you’re comfortable with pipelines and procedural generation.
- **Ultimate Challenge:** Save **Galactic Traders Online** for last, as it requires multiplayer infrastructure and long-term commitment.
